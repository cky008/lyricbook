# AI setlist research protocol

An AI agent preparing a preset must identify the artist, tour, city, date, and venue; prioritize official artist, promoter, venue, and ticketing sources; cross-check already-played shows; preserve original song-title spellings and aliases; and incorporate user-provided screenshots as separate evidence.

Every proposed song should be classified as confirmed, high-confidence, likely, rotation, encore candidate, or unverified. Save source references, retrieval time, confidence, and a concise evidence note. Produce multiple honest setlist variants when evidence conflicts. Never copy complete copyrighted lyrics into the public repository.
